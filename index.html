Common questions

How often should I service my AC?

Most units should be serviced at least once or twice a year. Dusty areas or heavy daily use need it more often.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes are dirty filters or coils, low gas from a leak, or a failing part. An inspection will find the real cause.

Do you work on commercial freezers and cold rooms?

Yes. We repair and maintain commercial fridges, display chillers, ice makers and cold rooms.

Will I know the price before work starts?

Yes. We inspect first and you agree the cost before we begin.
